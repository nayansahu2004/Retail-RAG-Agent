# NovaMart Warranty and Repairs Policy

## Manufacturer warranty
Every product carries the manufacturer warranty listed on its product page. You can raise warranty claims directly through the NovaMart service desk, so you do not need to contact the brand yourself.

## NovaMart Extended Protection
Extended Protection adds 1 or 2 extra years of coverage after the manufacturer warranty ends. It must be bought within 30 days of the original purchase. It costs 8% of the product price for 1 year or 14% for 2 years. It covers hardware and electrical failures, but not physical damage or liquid damage.

## Screen Shield accidental damage cover
Screen Shield covers accidental and liquid damage for smartphones and laptops. It costs ₹1,299 per year for smartphones and ₹2,999 per year for laptops. One claim is allowed per year, and each claim has a deductible of ₹999.

## How to raise a claim
Open the Support page, choose the order, and upload the invoice and photos of the issue. A pickup is arranged within 3 business days. Repairs take 10 to 15 business days. If a product cannot be repaired within 30 days, NovaMart replaces it with the same or an equivalent model.

## What warranty does not cover
Warranty and Extended Protection do not cover cosmetic scratches, damage from unauthorised repairs, software issues caused by third-party apps, consumables such as ear tips and straps, or products whose serial number has been removed.
