# NovaMart Customer Support

## Contact channels
NovaMart Support is available by chat on the Help page and by toll-free phone at 1800-123-4567, every day from 9 AM to 9 PM. Email support@novamart.example for anything that is not urgent; replies arrive within 24 hours.

## Questions the assistant cannot answer
For order-specific details such as your delivery status, refund status, invoice corrections or a live price check, contact NovaMart Support with your order ID. Agents can see your account; the assistant cannot.

## Escalation
If an issue is not resolved within 48 hours, ask the support agent to escalate it. A senior agent replies within 1 business day. Escalated warranty claims are reviewed by the service manager within 3 business days.
