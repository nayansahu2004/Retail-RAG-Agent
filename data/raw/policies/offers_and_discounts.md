# NovaMart Offers and Discounts

## Product discounts
Every product shows its MRP and NovaMart's selling price. The difference is the product discount, and it is already applied at checkout. No code is needed for it.

## Bank offers
HDFC Bank and ICICI Bank credit cards get a 10% instant discount, up to ₹3,000 per order, on orders of ₹15,000 or more. SBI debit cards get a 5% instant discount, up to ₹1,000 per order, on orders of ₹5,000 or more. Only one bank offer can be used per order. Bank offers can be combined with no-cost EMI.

## Coupon codes
WELCOME10 gives 10% off your first NovaMart order, up to ₹500. NOVA500 gives ₹500 off electronics orders of ₹10,000 or more (laptops, smartphones, televisions, smartwatches and headphones). FIT15 gives 15% off running shoes, up to ₹750. Only one coupon can be used per order, and coupons are applied to the selling price before any bank offer.

## Exchange offer
Old smartphones and laptops in working condition can be exchanged when buying a new smartphone or laptop. Exchange value is up to ₹15,000 for smartphones and up to ₹25,000 for laptops, depending on model and condition. The final value is confirmed by the courier at pickup and is deducted from the amount you pay.

## Student discount
Students who verify a college email ID get an extra 5% off laptops, up to ₹2,000, once per year. It can be combined with a bank offer but not with a coupon code.

## How offers combine
To get the maximum discount, offers are applied in this order: the selling price (product discount already included), then one coupon code, then one bank offer, then NovaCoins for Plus members (up to 20% of the order value). Exchange value is deducted after that. Price drop protection still applies after purchase.
