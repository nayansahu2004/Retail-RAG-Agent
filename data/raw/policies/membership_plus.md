# NovaMart Plus Membership

## Plans and pricing
NovaMart Plus costs ₹999 per year or ₹129 per month. Students with a valid college email ID get the annual plan for ₹499.

## Member benefits
Plus members get free standard delivery on all orders, free return pickups, early access to sales 24 hours before everyone else, priority customer support, and 5% back as NovaCoins on every order. NovaCoins earned are capped at ₹500 worth per month.

## Using NovaCoins
One NovaCoin equals ₹1. NovaCoins can pay for up to 20% of an order's value and expire 12 months after they are earned. NovaCoins cannot be transferred or exchanged for cash.

## Cancelling Plus
You can cancel Plus at any time from Account settings. Annual members who cancel within 14 days and have not used any benefit get a full refund. After that, the membership stays active until the end of the paid period and no partial refund is given.
