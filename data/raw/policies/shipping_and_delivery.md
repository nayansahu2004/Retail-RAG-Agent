# NovaMart Shipping and Delivery Policy

## Delivery timelines
Orders to metro cities are delivered in 1 to 2 business days. Other cities and towns take 3 to 5 business days. Remote pin codes, including parts of the North-East and island territories, can take up to 8 business days.

## Shipping charges
Standard delivery is free on orders of ₹499 or more. Orders below ₹499 have a ₹40 delivery charge. NovaMart Plus members get free standard delivery on every order with no minimum.

## Same-day express delivery
Express same-day delivery costs ₹149 and is available in Delhi, Mumbai, Bengaluru and Hyderabad for orders placed before 1 PM. Large appliances such as televisions are not eligible for express delivery.

## Cash on delivery
Cash on delivery is available for orders up to ₹50,000. A cash handling fee of ₹25 applies to every COD order. Orders above ₹50,000 must be prepaid.

## Television installation
Televisions include free installation by a certified technician within 48 hours of delivery. A wall-mount bracket costs ₹1,499 extra unless the product page says it is included in the box. Do not unbox the television before the technician arrives, or the free installation may be cancelled.

## Failed deliveries and tracking
Every order can be tracked from the Orders page. The courier makes 3 delivery attempts. If all 3 fail, the order is returned to NovaMart and a full refund is issued automatically.

## International shipping
NovaMart currently ships only within India. International addresses cannot be used at checkout.
