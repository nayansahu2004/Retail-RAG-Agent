# NovaMart Payments, EMI and Pricing Policy

## Accepted payment methods
NovaMart accepts UPI, credit cards, debit cards, net banking, the NovaMart Wallet, NovaMart PayLater and cash on delivery.

## No-cost EMI
No-cost EMI is available on orders above ₹10,000 using HDFC, ICICI, SBI or Axis Bank credit cards. Tenures of 3, 6 or 9 months are offered. The interest is given as an upfront discount, so the total you pay equals the product price.

## Standard EMI
Standard EMI with interest is available for up to 24 months on most credit cards. The bank charges its own interest rate and a one-time processing fee of ₹199.

## NovaMart PayLater
PayLater lets eligible customers buy now and pay within 30 days with no interest, up to a limit of ₹20,000. A late fee of ₹15 per day applies after the due date, capped at ₹500 per bill.

## Price drop protection
If the price of an item you bought drops on NovaMart within 7 days of delivery, the difference is credited to your NovaMart Wallet. You can claim it once per order from the Orders page.

## GST invoices
A GST invoice is available for every order on the Orders page. Businesses can add their GSTIN at checkout to claim input tax credit. A GSTIN cannot be added after the order is placed.
