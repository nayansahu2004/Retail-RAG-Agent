# NovaMart Returns and Refunds Policy

## Standard return window
Most items bought on NovaMart can be returned within 10 days of delivery. The item must be unused, in its original packaging, with all tags, manuals and accessories included. Category-specific rules below override this standard window.

## Electronics returns
Laptops, smartphones, televisions and smartwatches are eligible for a 7-day replacement only if they arrive damaged, defective or different from what was ordered. Change-of-mind returns for electronics are accepted within 7 days only if the manufacturer seal is unbroken. Once the seal is opened, a change-of-mind return is not possible, but the manufacturer warranty still applies.

## Footwear and bags
Running shoes and backpacks have an extended 30-day return window. Shoes must be unworn outdoors (trying them on indoors is fine) and bags must be unused with tags attached. Exchanges for a different size are free and can be requested twice per order.

## Non-returnable items
The following cannot be returned for change of mind: in-ear headphones and earbuds once the hygiene seal is opened, kitchen appliances such as air fryers once used, gift cards, and any item marked Final Sale. These items can still be replaced if they arrive damaged or defective.

## How refunds are processed
After you request a return, a pickup is scheduled within 2 business days. The item goes through a quality check that takes up to 48 hours after pickup. Approved refunds go back to the original payment method within 5 to 7 business days, and UPI refunds usually arrive within 2 to 3 business days. Cash-on-delivery orders are refunded instantly to the NovaMart Wallet, or to a bank account of your choice within 7 business days.

## Return pickup fees
Return pickups are free when the item is damaged, defective or wrong. For change-of-mind returns, a pickup fee of ₹99 is deducted from the refund. NovaMart Plus members never pay a return pickup fee.
